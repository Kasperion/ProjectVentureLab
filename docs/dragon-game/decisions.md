## Proposed design for review and rehearsal

The following details are candidates, not recorded owner approvals:

- Working encounter title: A Ring in the Hoard.
- Orryx, a proud and dryly funny dragon; Lena and Bram, the couple.
- The ring accidentally arrived in a gift basket and was mistaken for an intentional gift.
- The letter, inscription, repair, gift list, cloth, five coins, and drawing supplies.
- Success through evidence, a fair exchange, a reasoned appeal, a credible bluff, or another coherent approach.
- One location, one main character, and roughly 10–20 minutes on a first attempt.
- Typed freeform input with optional inspection controls and a notebook.
- One background and three consistent portrait expressions.
- One complete free introductory encounter followed by a paid pack.
- The production plan's $160 monthly allowance breakdown.

The [encounter design](encounter-design.md) gives the complete candidate scenario. Rehearsal can expose problems without turning every candidate detail into approved canon.
